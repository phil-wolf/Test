# What a bud bar is

## What a bud bar is

A bud bar is a mobile cannabis service for private events. You bring a staffed bar setup to the event, and trained staff guide guests through the cannabis part of the event.

Think of it like a mobile cocktail bar company, but for cannabis. Your product is the **service**: setup, staff, education, and a well-run guest experience.

## Services a bud bar offers

- Bar setup, display, and teardown
- Staff who explain product types, effects, and pacing to guests
- Rolling stations, accessory displays, and a set-up consumption area
- Add-ons: custom-branded accessories, a lounge area, coordination with the caterer
- Guided service of cannabis products at the bar [PHILIP TO VERIFY — what staff may handle depends on the state]

## Who hires a bud bar

- **Couples** planning weddings, engagement parties, and rehearsal dinners
- **Wedding and event planners** who need a reliable cannabis service provider to recommend
- **Event producers** running private parties, product launches, and brand activations
- **Corporate hosts** holding private company events and client gatherings
- **Venues and caterers** looking for a service provider they can refer

Planners and event producers matter most. They book many events a year, so one good relationship can mean steady work.

## How a bud bar differs from a dispensary

| | Bud bar | Dispensary |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Event service provider | Retail store |
| Where it operates | At the client's event | A fixed storefront |
| What the client pays for | Staff, setup, education, guest experience | Cannabis products |
| Who pays | The event host | Each shopper |
| Licensing | Varies by state and model [PHILIP TO VERIFY] | State cannabis retail license [PHILIP TO VERIFY] |

In short: a dispensary sells products. A bud bar sells a service at an event.
