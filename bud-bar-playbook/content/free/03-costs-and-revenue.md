- Business formation and registration
- Mobile bar, display, and signage
- Accessories and service supplies
- Branding, website, and booking tools

## Reading the numbers

- **Profit per event is before taxes** and before paying yourself for sales calls, travel, and admin time.
- **To raise profit:** add a per-guest fee, sell add-ons, set a minimum booking, or cut paid setup hours.
- **Watch for hidden costs:** unpaid hours, travel and parking, cancellations, and late payments.
- **Break-even:** startup costs ÷ profit per event = how many events it takes to earn back what you spent to start.
