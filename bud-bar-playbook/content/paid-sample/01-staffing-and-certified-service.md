## About this sample

## The roles at a bud bar event

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| Event lead | Runs the day: client and planner contact, venue check-in, timeline, staff breaks, and any incident decisions |
| Certified server | Staffs the bar, educates guests, paces service, and watches for guests who have had too much |
| Check-in / ID support | Confirms guest age before service [PHILIP TO VERIFY — ID rules vary by state] |
| Setup and runner | Loads in, sets up, restocks supplies, and breaks down |

At small events one person can cover two roles. The event lead role is never optional.

## How many staff to send

Example assumptions. Adjust these to your own events:

- **Up to 75 guests:** 1 event lead + 1 server
- **75–150 guests:** 1 event lead + 2 servers
- **150+ guests:** add 1 server for every 75 guests, plus a dedicated check-in person

Build staffing into every quote. Clients are paying for coverage, not just hours.

> Some venues and insurers set their own staffing minimums. [PHILIP TO VERIFY]

## Who to hire

Good places to find staff:

- Budtenders with product knowledge
- Bartenders and catering staff with event experience
- Hotel and venue hospitality staff

Look for people who stay calm under pressure, talk clearly with guests, and show up on time.

Before you hire, confirm:

- Whether staff should be employees or independent contractors in your state [PHILIP TO VERIFY]
- The minimum age for staff who work around cannabis [PHILIP TO VERIFY]

## Why every server should be THC ServePro certified

Certification means every server on your team has trained to the same written standard. That matters to the people who decide whether you get booked:

- **Insurers:** documented staff training can support your application and may affect coverage or price. [PHILIP TO VERIFY]
- **Venues:** they see a defined standard for guest service, pacing, and incident response before they let you in.
- **Planners and event producers:** they put their reputation on every service provider they recommend. Certification gives them something concrete to point to.

## Certified service as a selling point

How to use it to win bookings:

- Put **"All servers THC ServePro certified"** in proposals, on your website, and in your first call with every client.
- Offer venues and planners a staff roster with certification dates.
- Explain it in one sentence: "Every server is trained in responsible cannabis service, guest pacing, and incident response." [PHILIP TO VERIFY — confirm this matches the THC ServePro curriculum]
- **Do not overclaim.** Certification shows a standard of service. It does not make an event legal and it does not replace permits or insurance. [PHILIP TO VERIFY]
